CTET Syllabus 2025 helps candidates understand the structure of Paper 1 and Paper 2 before starting preparation. A clear understanding of the exam pattern and subject-wise topics improves time management and study planning.

## Exam Pattern Overview

- Objective type questions
- Fixed time duration for each paper
- Separate papers for primary and upper primary teaching eligibility
- Subject sections based on teaching level and specialization

## Main Subjects

- Child Development and Pedagogy
- Language I
- Language II
- Mathematics
- Environmental Studies
- Science or Social Studies for Paper 2

## Preparation Advice

Candidates should compare the latest syllabus with previous year topics and practice mock tests based on the current paper pattern.
