UP Police Admit Card 2025 is expected to be available before the examination date. Registered candidates should download their hall ticket using login credentials and verify all printed details in advance.

## Details to Check on Admit Card

- Candidate name
- Roll number
- Exam date and shift
- Reporting time
- Exam centre name and address
- Photograph and signature

## Documents to Carry

Candidates should carry the printed admit card along with a valid photo identity proof. Additional instructions mentioned by the board must also be followed.

## Exam Day Tips

- Reach the exam centre before reporting time
- Do not carry prohibited items
- Follow dress code or biometric instructions if mentioned
- Read all instructions printed on the admit card carefully

## Important Note

If any error appears on the admit card, contact the official helpdesk immediately. Use only the official board website for hall ticket download.
