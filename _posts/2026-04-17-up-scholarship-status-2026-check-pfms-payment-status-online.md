Students in Uttar Pradesh who applied for the UP Scholarship for the 2025-26 academic session can track their payment and application progress online. The most common way to check whether the scholarship amount has reached the bank account is through the PFMS portal, while the official state scholarship website helps students review their application status and verification progress.

## Overview

The UP Scholarship scheme supports pre-matric and post-matric students through direct financial assistance. Once the scholarship is approved, the amount is generally transferred through the Direct Benefit Transfer system, which sends the payment directly to the student's bank account.

Because payment and verification happen in stages, students should check both:

- the **PFMS portal** for payment tracking
- the **official UP Scholarship portal** for application and verification status

## Latest Update on UP Scholarship Status 2026

In many cases, payment depends on verification completion, district approval, and bank processing time. That is why some students may see updates earlier than others.

- tuition fees

For many students, this support plays an important role in continuing education without interruption.

## UP Scholarship Status 2026 Check Link

Students can check scholarship payment status through the PFMS "Know Your Payment" page:

- `https://pfms.nic.in/SitePages/KnowYourPayment_Dw_NewNew.aspx`

This link becomes useful once the payment file is processed and the scholarship transfer starts appearing in PFMS records.

## How to Check UP Scholarship Status 2026 on PFMS

Students can use the PFMS portal to check whether the scholarship amount has been sent or credited.

1. Visit `pfms.nic.in`.
2. Open the **Know Your Payment** section.
3. Enter your bank name and bank account number.
4. Re-enter the account number for confirmation.
5. Fill in the captcha code.
6. Complete OTP verification if the portal asks for it.
7. Submit the details to view the payment status.

The portal may show whether the payment is under processing, sent to the bank, or already disbursed.

## How to Check UP Scholarship Application Status 2026

Students can also verify the application stage through the official scholarship portal.

1. Visit `https://scholarship.up.gov.in`.
2. Open the **Status** section.
3. Select the academic year **2025-26**.
4. Enter the registration number or application ID.
5. Fill in the captcha code.
6. Submit the form.

After submission, the portal should display the current scholarship application or verification status.

## What Different Scholarship Status Messages Mean

Students often see different messages while checking PFMS or application status. These usually indicate different stages of the process.

### Processing

This means the scholarship payment request is still being processed by the authorities.

### Processing Under Bank

This usually means the payment file has been sent to the bank and the amount may be credited soon.

### Disbursed

This generally means the scholarship amount has been released by the government and is moving toward the student's bank account.

### Record Not Found

This may mean the payment data has not yet been updated on the PFMS portal or the entered bank details do not match the available records.

### Verified

On the scholarship application side, this usually means the verification process has been completed and the case is moving ahead for payment-related action.

### Pending or Rejected

This may indicate incomplete verification, missing documents, incorrect details, or some other issue requiring attention.

## What to Do If Your Scholarship Payment Is Delayed

Sometimes the scholarship amount takes longer than expected to reach the bank account. Common reasons include pending verification, incorrect bank details, mismatch in records, or bank-side processing delays.

If your payment is delayed:

- check your application status on the scholarship portal
- verify payment status on PFMS
- make sure your bank account is active and correctly linked
- confirm that Aadhaar and bank details match the submitted records where required
- contact your school or college for verification-related issues
- visit the District Social Welfare Office if the issue remains unresolved

## Important Tips for Students

- check both PFMS and the scholarship portal, not just one platform
- keep bank details and registration details ready before checking status
- use official portals only
- do not panic if the PFMS record is not visible immediately
- save screenshots or copies of status updates if any issue needs follow-up

## Frequently Asked Questions
