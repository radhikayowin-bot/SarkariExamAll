The 2026 India Post GDS recruitment drive aims to fill:

Candidates are shortlisted through a system-generated merit list based on Class 10 marks, including the conversion of grades or points into percentage marks up to four decimal places, as per the recruitment process.

## Official Websites to Check the Merit List

Candidates should monitor:

- `https://indiapostgdsonline.gov.in`

The state-wise merit list PDF is usually made available through the GDS Online Engagement portal.

## How the Merit List Is Prepared

According to the recruitment process referenced in the update, applicants are shortlisted on the basis of a system-generated merit list using marks obtained in the Secondary School Examination of Class 10 from recognized boards.

If grades or points are awarded instead of marks, they are converted into marks and then into percentage, calculated up to four decimal places.

## How to Download India Post GDS 3rd Merit List 2026

Once the third list is released, candidates can generally follow these steps:

1. Visit `indiapostgdsonline.gov.in`.
2. Open the results or shortlisted candidates section.
3. Click the relevant link for the GDS 2026 supplementary or third merit list.
4. Select the concerned state or circle, if required.
5. Open and download the PDF merit list.
6. Search your registration number or name in the list.

Candidates should download only the official state-wise PDF and avoid depending on re-uploaded copies from unrelated websites.

## How to Check the Second Merit List Reference

The available update also notes that candidates could check the second merit list by visiting the official India Post website, opening the GDS Online Engagement section, and then selecting the supplementary list link for the relevant state.

This same process is likely to remain the main route for checking the third list as well.

## Documents Needed for Verification

Candidates shortlisted in the merit list should keep these documents ready for document verification:

- Class 10 marksheet
- valid identity proof
- self-attested copies of required documents
- caste certificate, if applicable
- EWS certificate, if applicable
- medical certificate
- domicile or tribal certificate, where required
- passport-size photographs
- printout of the application form

Candidates should also carry original documents along with photocopies if the official notice asks for both.

## Important Verification Advice

If your name appears in the merit list, do not wait until the last date to arrange documents. Verification delays often happen because of missing certificates, mismatched personal details, or incomplete photocopy sets.

To stay prepared:

- verify your name, category, and educational details in all documents
- keep original and photocopy sets ready
- check the divisional office and reporting deadline carefully
- read the official notice for any state-specific instruction
