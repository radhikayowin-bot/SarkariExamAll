National Scholarship Portal 2025 offers application access for eligible students under central and state scholarship schemes. Students should check income limit, category rules, academic qualification, and renewal conditions before applying.

## Common Eligibility Factors

- Student must belong to an eligible scheme category
- Academic qualification should meet scheme requirements
- Family income must fall within the permitted range
- Required bank and identity details should be available

## How to Apply

- Register on the official NSP portal
- Complete profile details
- Select the correct scholarship scheme
- Upload required documents
- Submit and verify final application status

## Important Reminder

Read the scheme-specific notice carefully because required documents and deadlines may vary.
