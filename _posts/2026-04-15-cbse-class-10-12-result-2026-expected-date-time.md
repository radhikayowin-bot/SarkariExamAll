## Alternate Ways to Check CBSE Result

Apart from official websites, CBSE also provides multiple ways to access results:

- DigiLocker  
- UMANG App  
- SMS Services  
- School Notice Boards  

👉 These options are useful if the website is slow due to heavy traffic.
