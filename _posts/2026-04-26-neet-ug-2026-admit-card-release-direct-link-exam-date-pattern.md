The NEET UG 2026 admit card is expected to be released shortly by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates who have registered for the exam will be able to download their hall ticket from the official website once the link becomes active.

Students should stay alert and keep their login credentials ready to avoid last-minute issues while downloading the admit card.

## Overview

NEET UG is one of India’s most competitive medical entrance exams conducted for admission to MBBS, BDS, and other undergraduate medical courses. The admit card is a mandatory document that must be carried to the examination centre.

Without the hall ticket, candidates will not be allowed to appear in the exam.

## NEET UG 2026 Admit Card Release Date

As per the latest update, the admit card is expected to be released in the last week of April 2026. However, the exact time of release may vary, so candidates are advised to regularly check the official website.

## Official Website to Download Admit Card

Candidates can download their NEET UG 2026 admit card from:

- https://neet.nta.nic.in  

Any official update or additional links will also be published on this portal.

## How to Download NEET UG Admit Card 2026

Follow these simple steps:

1. Visit the official NEET website  
2. Click on the “NEET UG 2026 Admit Card” link  
3. Enter your application number and password  
4. Submit the details  
5. Download and print the admit card  

Make sure to take a clear printout for exam day use.

## Details Mentioned on NEET Admit Card

After downloading the hall ticket, carefully verify all details:

- Candidate’s name  
- Roll number  
- Application number  
- Exam centre name and address  
- Centre code  
- Reporting time  
- Exam timing  
- Important instructions  

If any information is incorrect, immediately contact the NTA helpline or exam authorities.

## NEET UG 2026 Exam Date & Timing

The NEET UG 2026 examination is expected to be conducted in **early May 2026** in offline (pen-and-paper) mode.

- Exam duration: 3 hours  
- Timing: Afternoon session (generally 2 PM to 5 PM)

Candidates should reach the exam centre well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.

## NEET UG 2026 Exam Pattern

The exam pattern remains consistent with previous years:

- Total Questions: 180  
- Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology (Botany & Zoology)  
- Total Marks: 720  
- Mode: Offline (OMR-based)  
- Duration: 180 minutes  

Each correct answer carries marks, while negative marking may apply for incorrect responses as per NTA guidelines.
