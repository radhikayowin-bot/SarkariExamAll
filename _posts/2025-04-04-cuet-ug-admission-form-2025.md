CUET UG Admission Form 2025 is important for students seeking undergraduate admission in participating universities. Applicants should complete registration within the official schedule and keep all required documents ready.

## Documents Commonly Needed

- Passport size photo
- Signature
- Class 10 and 12 details
- Identity proof
- Category or reservation certificate if applicable

## Key Points

- Verify university and course eligibility before applying
- Check subject selection rules carefully
- Review application fee before final payment
- Save the submitted form and payment receipt

Students should monitor correction window dates, exam city options, and counseling announcements after form submission.
