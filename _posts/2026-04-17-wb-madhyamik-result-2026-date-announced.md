## Official Result Date

WBBSE has stated that the Madhyamik Pariksha 2026 result will be published on:

- **Date:** 8 May 2026
- **Day:** Friday

The board has also indicated that further result access details will be shared separately through official communication.

## What the Board Said

As referenced in the board notice, the result for Madhyamik Pariksha 2026 will be published on 8 May 2026. The board also advised examinees to stay connected only with official communications for timely updates about result access and related instructions.

## Exam Timeline and Student Participation

The Madhyamik examination for 2026 was conducted from 2 February to 12 February across West Bengal.

According to the information referenced in the update:

- around 9.71 lakh candidates appeared for the examination
- exams were held at 2,682 centres across the state
- 5,44,606 candidates were female
- 4,26,733 candidates were male
- one candidate was listed in the transgender category

The result is scheduled to be declared 85 days after the conclusion of the examination period, reflecting a relatively quick evaluation cycle for such a large state-level exam.
