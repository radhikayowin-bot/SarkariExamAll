CBSE Class 10 Result 2026 is expected to be released soon by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). As per reports, the board may announce the results on April 15, 2026. Students will be able to check their results online through the official CBSE websites once released.

According to sources, CBSE has completed all necessary preparations and may release the result with only a short prior notice.

## Important Highlights

- Expected result date: April 15, 2026
- Exam start date: February 17, 2026
- Class 10 exam end date: March 11, 2026
- Class 12 exam end date: April 10, 2026
- Minimum passing marks: 33%

## Official Websites To Check Result

Students can check their results on the following official websites:

- https://results.cbse.nic.in
- https://cbse.gov.in
- https://cbse.nic.in

## How To Download CBSE Class 10 Result 2026

Follow these steps to download your marksheet:

1. Visit the official website https://results.cbse.nic.in  
2. Click on "Secondary School Examination (Class X) 2026"  
3. Enter required details:
   - Roll Number  
   - School Number  
   - Admit Card ID  
   - Date of Birth  
4. Click on "Submit"  
5. Your result will appear on the screen  
6. Download and save it for future reference  

## Check Result Via SMS

Students can also check their results through SMS:

- Type: `cbse10` or `cbse12`
- Add your Roll Number, School Code, and Centre Number
- Send to: **7738299899**

## Other Platforms To Check Result

CBSE results will also be available on:

- DigiLocker (Parinam Manjusha)
- UMANG Mobile App

## Grace Marks & Passing Criteria

Students who fall short by one or two marks may be awarded grace marks. To pass the examination, candidates must secure at least **33% marks overall**.

## Final Advice

Students are advised to keep their admit cards ready while checking results. In case of heavy traffic on websites, try alternative platforms like DigiLocker or SMS service.

Always verify details mentioned in the marksheet and contact your school in case of discrepancies.
