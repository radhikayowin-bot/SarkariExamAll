## Information You May Need Before Checking

Depending on the result portal, students may be asked to enter:

- roll number
- school number
- admit card ID
- date of birth or another verification detail

Keeping these details ready helps avoid panic when the result page becomes busy.

## How to Download the Result

1. Visit an official CBSE result website.
2. Open the Class 10 result link.
3. Enter the required credentials carefully.
4. Submit the form.
5. View the marksheet and save a copy.

If the website slows down, avoid repeated refresh attempts. Waiting for a few minutes is often more effective.

## Other Ways to Check CBSE Result

Students may also be able to use:

- DigiLocker
- UMANG app
- SMS service, if officially activated for the result cycle

These alternatives are useful when the main website has heavy traffic.

## What to Check on the Scorecard

After downloading the result, review these details carefully:

- student name and personal information
- subject-wise marks
- total marks
- pass status
- any discrepancy in subject entries or spelling

If anything appears incorrect, contact the school or follow the official CBSE verification process instead of depending on social media advice.
