UPSC Civil Services Examination 2025 notification is one of the most important updates for candidates preparing for IAS, IPS, IFS, and other top civil services. Candidates should read the official notice carefully before registration.

## Eligibility Overview

- Bachelor degree from a recognized university
- Age limit as per UPSC rules
- Category-wise relaxation for eligible candidates
- Nationality conditions as mentioned in the official notification

## Selection Stages

- Preliminary Examination
- Main Examination
- Personality Test or Interview
- Final merit list

## Why This Notification Matters

This examination opens the path to multiple prestigious services. Missing the registration deadline, eligibility detail, or document requirement can create avoidable problems later.

## Final Note

Candidates should use the official UPSC website for registration, syllabus, exam calendar, and correction-related notices.
