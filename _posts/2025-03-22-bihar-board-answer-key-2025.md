Bihar Board Answer Key 2025 has been issued for candidate review. Students and applicants should check the provisional answer key carefully and submit objections within the deadline if they find any discrepancy.

## What to Review

- Question number and answer option
- Subject code and paper set
- Official objection dates
- Supporting proof required for challenge

## Objection Process

Candidates usually need to login to the official portal, select the question to challenge, upload supporting explanation if required, and submit the objection before the final date.

## Final Answer Key

After reviewing objections, the board may release a revised or final answer key. Final result preparation is generally based on the final answer key only.
