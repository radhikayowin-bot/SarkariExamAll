## Editorial Role

SarkariExamAll is maintained under a central editorial workflow that reviews job, result, admit card, scholarship, answer key, and exam-related updates before or after publication depending on the type of content.

The site editor role focuses on keeping the website useful, readable, and as accurate as possible for students and applicants who depend on official process information.

## What the Editorial Role Covers

The editorial process typically includes:

- checking the authority or board name
- reviewing dates, links, and key instructions
- improving structure and readability
- clarifying whether a date is confirmed or still expected
- updating posts when better official information becomes available

## Editorial Scope

SarkariExamAll is an independent informational website. It is not a government office, recruiting board, university authority, or official result portal.

The site editorial role is therefore limited to publishing, reviewing, and improving informational summaries. Final action must always be taken through the original official authority.
