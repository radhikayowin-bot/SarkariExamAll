## Purpose

SarkariExamAll publishes informational updates based on official notices, authority portals, and publicly available examination or recruitment information. This page explains how we approach sourcing and why users should still confirm final details through the original authority.

## Sources We Prefer

We try to rely first on:

- official government department websites
- recruitment boards and commissions
- universities and public education institutions
- official result portals
- information bulletins, notices, and published schedules

## How We Use Source Material

Our role is to summarize and organize important information in a clearer format for readers. We do not replace the official notification, recruitment notice, board circular, or department portal.

Whenever possible, we try to identify:

- the responsible authority
- the relevant official website
- the action readers need to take
- the stage at which information is confirmed, expected, or still awaited

## What We Avoid

We try not to present rumors, unverified social media claims, or unsupported dates as confirmed updates.

If an expected date or timeline is being discussed but has not been officially declared, we try to say that clearly.

## Why Readers Should Still Check the Official Source

Recruitment, result, scholarship, and exam-related information can change quickly. Even when a summary is accurate at the time of publishing, the official source remains the final authority for:

- eligibility
- deadlines
- fees
- documents
- correction windows
- final links and instructions

## If You Notice a Source Issue

If a page appears outdated, unclear, or inconsistent with an official notice, please contact us through the [Contact page](/contact/). We review legitimate correction requests as part of our editorial and corrections process.
