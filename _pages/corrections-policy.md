## Our Approach to Corrections

We aim to keep every page as accurate and current as possible, but errors can still happen in fast-moving exam, result, admit card, scholarship, and recruitment updates.

When an issue is identified, we review the page and correct it when appropriate.

## What We Correct

We may update a page if it contains:

- an incorrect date
- a broken or outdated official link
- a factual error in eligibility, process, or document details
- a misleading summary that does not match the official notice
- an outdated expected timeline after an official confirmation is released

## How to Report an Issue

If you find an error, please contact us with:

- the page URL
- the issue you noticed
- the correct official source, if available
- a brief explanation

You can send correction-related messages through the [Contact page](/contact/) or by using the editorial contact email listed there.

## Review Process

When a correction request is received, we try to:

1. review the relevant page
2. compare the reported issue with official information
3. update the content if the correction is valid

Not every reported issue results in a visible change, but legitimate factual concerns should be reviewed.

## Time to Update

Correction timing depends on the type of issue and the availability of official confirmation. High-impact errors such as incorrect dates, dead official links, or process-level misinformation are prioritized first.

## Scope

SarkariExamAll provides informational summaries only. We cannot correct or influence records held by government departments, boards, universities, banks, or recruitment authorities.

For official action, users must contact the relevant authority directly.
