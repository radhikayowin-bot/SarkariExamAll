You can contact SarkariExamAll for correction requests, report updates, advertising inquiries, or general support.

**Response time:** We aim to respond to all messages within 2-3 business days.

**Editorial inquiries:** contact@sarkariexamall.com

**Business inquiries:** admin@sarkariexamall.com

## When to Contact Us

Please contact us if you want to report:

- an incorrect exam, result, or application date
- a broken official link
- missing eligibility or document details
- outdated scholarship, admit card, or answer key information
- a factual correction request

## Before You Contact Us

To help us review your message faster, include:

- the page URL
- the issue you found
- the correct official source, if available
- a short explanation of what needs to be updated

## Editorial and Policy Pages

For more information about how we publish and revise content, please see:

- [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/)
- [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/)

We do not provide official government processing support, application approval, result modification, or payment release services. For final action, users should always follow the relevant official authority.
